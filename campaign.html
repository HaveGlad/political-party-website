Fundraising

In order to fundraise, we will sell party merchandise, such as clothing items that contain our logo and our slogan, bags, keychains, etc. We will also participate at community events and political meetings to advocate for our policies and develop deeper connections with the members of the community. To further interact with the community and to encourage donations, we will organize events such as rallies, auctions, to connect with more members of the community, which increases donations. We will also reach out through social media, ads, email lists, and networking in order to contact possible donors and receive valuable allies.

Recruitment

To recruit staff and volunteers, we will be participating in lots of community events in order to interact with the community members and make personal connections with them. We will also use media to gain attention, which leads to more people learning about our party and getting interested in it. To recruit staff specifically, we will reach out to specific people who we feel represent our values well and would be dedicated to the betterment of the Main Protectors. Additionally, we will ask dedicated volunteers to play a greater role in our party. Through all of these channels, we will be able to recruit party members and have a solid staff that will support our party.

Target audience & messaging

Our target audience are workers, young to middle aged people and those who want protection. By focusing on this specific audience, our party will directly appeal to everyday citizens who feel left behind by big corporations, and businesses. We want to reach young adults who are trying to afford their first homes, and factory workers who want to keep their jobs within our countries borders. Our message is designed to feel completely reassuring, simple and direct so that people know that they have a group that is entirely dedicated to looking out for them. Every single speech, social media post, and flyer we create will repeat these phrases, “We are here to protect you”, “We’re here for you”, “Americans first” to build strong trust with our voters. By keeping our messages centered on safety, job stability, and national care, we will make people feel completely supported against outside threats.

Media Usage

To get the most people to notice us we will use a mix of social media and commercials on the TV. On social media we will post short infographics about our ten platform planks. Along with updates on our campaign, recruitments, volunteers, fundraisers and donations. By actively posting things, younger voters, and those who use social media will be able to see how they can get involved and buy our custom gear. We will also post short video clips about our work, and policies on TikTok, and Instagram. For the TV commercials we want to focus on those who don’t use social media as much. These commercials will show our workers talking to everyday workers and explaining how we plan to protect local neighborhoods and families.
