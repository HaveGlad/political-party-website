10 Platform planks on social, economic, foreign, and domestic issues

Immigration

We welcome immigrants to the US. If people want to migrate to the US, they need to go through the proper channels of the legal immigration systems to get here, and then follow the laws of this country while they are here.

Healthcare

We believe that everyone should have access to good medical care, without it being so expensive. We want to make sure that regular doctor visits are affordable and lower the price of medical treatments.

Inflation

We need to stop the rising prices of groceries, gas, and everyday items that are hurting families. We want to stop the government's wasteful spending and help people keep the money they earned from working by getting rid of unnecessary rules that make things super expensive.

Employment

We believe that citizens should be hard working and that employees should be treated fairly. We also believe that employers should offer more job opportunities, specifically more entry-level job opportunities.

Equal Rights

We believe that every single person should be treated equally, and have the same protection under the law as everyone else. We want to enforce anti-discrimination rules in schools, work, and housing to make sure everyone gets a fair shot.

Abortion

We believe that abortion disrespects the preciousness of human life. Overall, abortion should not be the answer; only under extreme circumstances (such as the mother’s life endangered) is it allowable.

Housing

We want everyone to be able to afford housing, and we pledge to lower the cost of housing. We also believe in the importance of low-density housing and the culture that creates.

Global Trade

We focus on trade that is fair and will look out for our U.S. factories and workers. We want to stop foreign countries from taking advantage of our markets, by protecting our factory workers and producing more needed goods here.

Foreign Policy

We prioritize the well being of our own citizens. We also prioritize the stability of our country. We promise to take care of our citizens and our country first before helping other countries.

Public Safety

We want everyone to feel safe in their communities. We want to build trust with local authorities and the people in the community. To do this we need to give first responders better training to handle things, and fund emergency services, so that they have the resources to help during a crisis.
