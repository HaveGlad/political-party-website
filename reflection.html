Haven's reflection

My partner was Coco Brown. There wasn't really a challenge in making a unified platform since we both have pretty similar political views, but something that was challenging in making a platform was really knowing where to start and putting what we learned from the book into action, but also in more detail. In my role as manager, I had to focus more on the logistics of the political party, such as fundraising and recruitment. To do this, I had to do research about specific ways political parties obtain funding, which was very informative. Coco was the president, and she focused more on the platform and the specific policies she was running for. The main difference between our roles is my role involved more analytical thinking while Coco's role involved more ideological thinking. This experience taught me a lot of the specifics of how political parties are formed and run, and that it's not as easy as it sounds. I also learned about the importance of collaboration and of really putting your ideas out there. I also learned the importance of not being afraid of what you believe in, and just standing up for what you believe.

Coco's reflection

My partner for this assignment was HG. The most challenging part about creating a unified platform was deciding what we both wanted. It was hard to figure out what our ten platform planks would be, one reason was that we didn’t know what it was. But once we came up with our ideas we both agreed these would be good. After looking at our ten planks I thought the whole thought was about protection, she thought it was a good idea and we went with it. HG came up with the color, green, because we thought it was a good color to signify protection. It was hard to come up with a good slogan, but once HG had typed it as one of the options I thought it was really good. I wanted it to do something with protection, and she really liked the fair shot, so this option was the best for what we both wanted. She was a great partner to work with, and we were able to easily work together and decide the campaign strategies. Haven was the manager so she focused on the statistics, while I as the candidate focused on the campaign itself. This project has taught me about how real political parties function in real life. It’s shown me that parties aren’t just one person making all the rules, it's built on teamwork, compromise, communication, and blending different ideas together.
